A rare opportunity to acquire Benevenagh, a magnificent 4-bedroom end of terrace Victorian residence in the heart of Greystones Village, one of the town's most desirable locations, The Old Burnaby.

Benevenagh is a home of exceptional character and fascinating history, having been the former home of legendary Irish musician Ronnie Drew, founding member of The Dubliners. This wonderful connection adds a unique sense of provenance to an already special property.

Beautifully renovated while retaining its original Victorian character, the property combines generous proportions, high ceilings and an abundance of period detail with a superb contemporary specification. The result is a warm, elegant and highly individual family home presented in excellent condition.

Occupying one of the village's most sought-after addresses, Benevenagh places boutique shops, cafés, award-winning restaurants, the marina, beaches, harbour and DART station all within a short stroll. Combining the charm of a thriving coastal village with outstanding convenience, Greystones continues to be one of Ireland's most sought-after places to call home.

Accommodation

Hall

Bright and welcoming, with a feature original hardwood staircase, custom-made Alan Brown seating/storage bench, attractive herringbone flooring, cast-iron radiator, understairs storage and attractive ceiling coving with wall panelling throughout

Sitting Room

A beautifully proportioned south-facing reception room featuring upgraded double-glazed sash windows and wall panelling throughout. High ceiling with ornate coving and centre rose. Feature original fireplace with wood-burning stove and slate hearth. Traditional cast-iron radiator.

Living Room

Interconnecting with the sitting room, this elegant reception room features an Alan Brown fitted alcove units with smart lighting. Ornate coving and ceiling rose, and a fireplace with slate inset and brass-trimmed surround incorporating a cast-iron wood burning stove. Traditional cast-iron radiator.

Kitchen / Dining / Family Area

A superb open-plan space and the true heart of the home, showcasing a state-of-the-art Alan Brown kitchen with custom-built banquette seating and work/homework built in unit. Wide plank wood flooring. The kitchen features polished stone worktops and a centre island incorporating a Bora hob with built-in extractor fan.

A comfortable lounge area within the open-plan space is currently used as a third family room and features a gas stove. Sliding door with electric motorised blinds leads directly to the well-maintained garden and stone patio, creating an excellent space for family living and alfresco dining. The kitchen provides an extensive range of high- and low-level units, top-of-the-range appliances and cleverly designed pantry.
